## Slide 2: Problem (0:05)

**Founders make claims. Nobody checks them in time.**

- "50,000 customers." "The only platform." "$40B market."
- Checked by hand, days later, or never
- A chat answer can't be shown to partners: no source per sentence

## Slide 3: What Proven does (backdrop for 0:15 to 1:50)

**Paste a URL. Get a verdict per claim, with the quote.**

- Pitch Audit: 8 claims checked against independent sources in 23 s
- Deep memo: Claims vs. reality, 17 of 17 lines verified
- Told lenders $220m in sales. The audit found about $55m.
- Live Room: a spoken claim checked in about 8 s

## Slide 4: How Telem makes it possible (backdrop for 1:50 to 2:15)

**Telem picks the engines. Proven learns which ones hold up.**

- A topic and an engine list for every query, shown in the trail
- 16 engines, 20 queries in one deep memo, one API
- LinkedIn, X, Reddit, YouTube, Trustpilot, Product Hunt, WeChat
- Pages more engines agree on come first; news kept to the last year
- Monitor agent watches the company after the memo
- An engine earns credit only when its page backs a verified line
